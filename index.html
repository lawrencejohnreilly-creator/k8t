K8T is a conversational AI prototype built on twenty-five IETF Internet-Drafts and the live instruments behind them. Switch to Borrower and try to make it quote a rate, promise an approval, or repeat a Social Security number. It will not, and the right-hand channel shows you exactly which control stopped it, under which regulation, on which policy version. Every turn is attested, including the clean ones.

Independent prototype by Lawrence John Reilly Jr. Not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by Symple Lending. Policy text is illustrative and is not approved compliance language.

Answers are grounded in a fixed corpus. Uncited claims are flagged, not hidden.
